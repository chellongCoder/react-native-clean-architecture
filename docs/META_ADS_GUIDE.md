# Guide to Managing and Advertising Your Meta Horizon App

This guide clarifies the two-platform process for managing and promoting your Meta Horizon application. You will use the **Meta Developer Center** to configure your app and the **Meta Ads Manager** to run paid ad campaigns.

---

## Part 1: Configuring Your App in the Meta Developer Center

The [Meta Horizon Developer Center](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/manage/applications) is where you manage your app's core settings, submissions, and technical details. Before you can effectively advertise your app, you need to ensure it's properly set up here.

**Key Steps in the Developer Center:**

1.  **App Registration:** Your app must be registered here. This will generate an **App ID**, which is crucial for identifying your app across Meta's services.
2.  **Configuration:** Fill out all required information for your application, such as:
    *   App details (name, description, icons).
    *   Platform settings (e.g., for Rift, Quest).
    *   API and SDK settings. This is where you'll find credentials if your app uses Meta APIs.
3.  **App Submission:** Use this dashboard to submit your app for review to be published on the Meta Quest Store or App Lab. An app generally needs to be approved and live before you can run install campaigns for it.
4.  **Analytics:** Review basic performance metrics and analytics for your app provided by Meta.

**Think of this as your app's "home base" for all technical and administrative settings.**

---

## Part 2: Creating an Ad Campaign in Meta Ads Manager

Once your app is set up and preferably live on the store, you use a separate platform, **Meta Ads Manager**, to run paid campaigns to drive downloads and engagement.

**Step-by-Step Guide to Creating Your Campaign:**

### 1. Access Meta Ads Manager

*   Log in to your Facebook account and navigate to [**Meta Ads Manager**](https://business.facebook.com/adsmanager).

### 2. Create a New Campaign

*   Click the green **+ Create** button.

### 3. Choose Your Campaign Objective

*   For driving downloads and engagement with your app, select **App Promotion**.

### 4. Name Your Campaign

*   Give your campaign a clear, descriptive name (e.g., `ABeeCi - Quest - US - Install Campaign - Q1 2026`).

### 5. Configure the Ad Set (Targeting & Budget)

*   **App:** Select your app using the **App ID** from the Developer Center.
*   **Budget & Schedule:** Set a **daily** or **lifetime** budget and choose a start/end date.
*   **Audience:** Define who you want to see your ads. This is the most critical step. You can target by:
    *   **Location:** Countries, regions, etc.
    *   **Demographics:** Age and gender.
    *   **Interests & Behaviors:** Target users based on interests relevant to your app (e.g., "VR Gaming," "Education Tech," "Fitness Apps").
*   **Placements:** Choose where your ads appear. **Advantage+ Placements** is recommended to let Meta optimize delivery across Facebook, Instagram, and other placements.

### 6. Design Your Ad (The "Creative")

*   **Format:** Video is highly effective for showing app gameplay or features. You can also use single images.
*   **Media:** Upload your video or image assets.
*   **Text & Headline:** Write compelling copy that highlights your app's unique value.
*   **Call to Action (CTA):** Choose **Install Now** or **Download**. This will link directly to your app's page on the Meta Quest Store.

### 7. Review and Publish

*   Double-check all settings and click **Publish**. Your ad will be reviewed by Meta before it goes live.

---

## Summary

*   **Manage** your app's settings and submissions in the **Developer Center**.
*   **Advertise** your app to new users through **Ads Manager**.
